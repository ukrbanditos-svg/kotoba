Privacy Policy.

A clear overview of how Kotoba: Japanese From Zero handles information. Last updated: October 10, 2026.

1. At a glance

Kotoba is an educational app for learning Japanese. The current native iOS version does not require an account, display ads, or use its own server to store your learning information.

Local-first learning: lessons and vocabulary are bundled with the app, and your study progress is saved locally on your device. Kotoba does not send your learning history to the developer.

2. Information stored by the app

Kotoba stores learning preferences and progress on your iPhone using Apple's local app storage. This can include:

  • completed lessons and your current lesson position;
  • exercise results and answers to review;
  • flashcard review status, favorites, and study activity;
  • your selected interface language and whether onboarding was completed.

This information is used to show your progress and continue your learning experience. Based on the current reviewed source code, the app does not transmit these records to a Kotoba-operated backend.

3. Audio and device permissions

Japanese pronunciation playback uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech functionality. Kotoba does not record your voice or upload voice recordings. Speaking practice asks you to repeat phrases aloud; it does not automatically evaluate microphone input in the current version.

Operating-system speech features and device services may be subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

4. Backup, export, and deletion

When you choose to export your progress, Kotoba creates a JSON backup file that you control. You can save, share, or import this file using standard iOS file controls. If you share a backup with someone else, they may be able to read the information it contains.

You can remove locally saved data by deleting the app. Files you exported separately will not be deleted automatically, and iOS device backups may retain copies under Apple's backup settings.

5. Our website

This informational website does not include advertising scripts, analytics trackers, or a user login. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical connection data such as IP addresses and request logs in line with its privacy statement.

If you contact us for support, the information you voluntarily include in your message may be used to reply and address your request. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal details.

6. Children and families

Kotoba is designed for language learners and does not ask for personal profiles. If a parent or guardian has a privacy question, please use the support page.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Kotoba gains new features. Any material changes to data handling will be reflected here, and App Store privacy disclosures should be updated as needed.

For questions about this policy, visit our Support page.

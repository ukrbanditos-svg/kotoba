Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know Japanese before starting?
No. Kotoba is designed for absolute beginners. The lessons introduce sentence structures and useful words from the ground up.
Can I learn without an internet connection?
The native app bundles its lessons and vocabulary for offline use. Pronunciation playback depends on the Japanese speech voices available in iOS.
How do I back up my learning progress?
In Kotoba, open More → Save / Transfer Progress and choose Export Progress. Save the JSON file somewhere safe. To restore it, use the import option in the same section. Importing replaces the current progress, so export a copy first.
Can I switch between English and Russian?
Yes. Open the More tab and select your preferred language. Your completed lessons and review progress are shared between the two language interfaces.
Does the app check my spoken Japanese using a microphone?
Not yet. You can listen and repeat Japanese examples, but the current app does not score your pronunciation using microphone recordings.
How do I report an error in a lesson?
Send the lesson number, the phrase or exercise involved, and what seems incorrect by email using the contact address above. This makes it easier to reproduce and fix.
How do I delete my progress?
Learning progress is stored locally. Deleting the app generally removes the app's local data. Any exported backup files or iOS backups are separate and may need to be deleted individually.
Will more lessons be added?
The creator is learning Japanese alongside the community and plans to improve Kotoba over time. Future content and features are not guaranteed on a fixed schedule.
